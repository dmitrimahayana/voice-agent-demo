Dewlane Skin Studio: frequently asked questions (fictional demo studio)

Is this a medical clinic?
No. Dewlane is a beauty skin care studio with licensed estheticians. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions. For moles, infections, prescription medication or a skin condition, please see a doctor or dermatologist.

Which treatment is right for me?
Our estheticians can tell you at a free 30-minute skin consultation. Over the phone, Elena can suggest a treatment based on your skin type and concerns.

How often should I come?
Facials every 4 to 6 weeks. Peels every 4 weeks as a series of 3. Microneedling every 4 to 6 weeks as a series of 3.

Will I be red after a treatment?
Facials: little or no redness. Peels: mild redness and light flaking for 2 to 4 days. Microneedling: redness like a mild sunburn for 1 to 2 days.

Can I wear makeup after?
After facials and dermaplaning, yes. After peels and microneedling, wait 24 hours.

Can I combine vitamin C and retinol?
Many people use vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night. Stop retinol before peels, microneedling and dermaplaning as listed in each treatment's preparation.

Do you treat men and teens?
Yes. Guests under 16 need a parent or guardian present.

Gift cards?
Yes, at the front desk or by phone, in any amount.

Languages
We welcome guests in English and Spanish.
