Book by voice with Elena

Press Start a call in the panel at the bottom right of this page, then allow your microphone.

She asks about your skin type and what bothers you most. Before a peel or microneedling she checks whether you're pregnant or taking isotretinoin, because those treatments aren't safe then.

Then she offers two open times, spells your email back to you, and sends the reservation with how to prepare. You can also ask her to move or cancel a booking made with your email.
